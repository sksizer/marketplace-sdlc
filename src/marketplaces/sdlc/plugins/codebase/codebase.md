---
name: codebase
description: Skills for understanding and improving an unfamiliar body of code.
ap-kind: plugin
ap-version: 0.1.0
---

Skills that operate on a codebase as a whole rather than on a change to it:
getting oriented in an unfamiliar repository, finding where something is
implemented, and surfacing what the code's own shape suggests about what it
needs next.

Nothing here assumes a project layout, a planning system, or a CLI beyond git.
