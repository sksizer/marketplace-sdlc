# Hello world

The smallest possible skill. It exists to prove one thing: that a skill authored
in this vault reached a harness through the `sdlc` marketplace.

## What to do

Greet the user, then report three facts so they can confirm the install:

- The skill's name — `hello-world`.
- The plugin it came from — `demo`.
- The marketplace that served it — `sdlc`.

Say them in one short sentence. No preamble, no offer of further help.

## Example

> Hello — `hello-world` here, from the `demo` plugin of the `sdlc` marketplace.
> The install worked.
